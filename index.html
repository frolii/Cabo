Cards & Scoring Rules

🎯 Calling "CABO" & Penalties

  • Successful Call: If the player who called Cabo has the strictly lowest (or tied for lowest) raw card sum, they score 0 points for the round!
  • Failed Call: If another player has a strictly lower card sum, the Cabo caller fails and receives a +10 point penalty (either Card Sum + 10 in official rules, or Flat +10 depending on your setup toggle).
  • Other Players: Always score the sum of their remaining cards.

🦄 The "12-12-13-13" Rule (Shooting the Moon)

If any player finishes the round holding both 12s and both 13s (raw sum of 50):

  • Official Rule: That player scores 0 points, and every other player receives +50 points!
  • Minus-50 Variant: Alternatively, toggle the -50 pts variant in Setup so that player deducts 50 points from their total.

💥 Exact Threshold Reset ("Kamikaze")

If a player's cumulative total lands on exactly 100 points (or 50 points in Speed Mode), their total score immediately drops back down to 50 (or 25) once per game instead of ending the game!
